About

Matthew Valancy is a Silicon Valley engineer working in robotics, simulation, perception, and edge AI. These pages collect his teaching material: complete lesson plans, interactive guides, and study resources — all free, all written for people meeting the subject for the first time.

The through-line is ownership. Capable AI is moving onto ordinary phones, tablets, and laptops, and the people who understand what these systems are made of will be the ones who decide how they get used — at home, at work, and in their communities. These courses are written to put you on that side of the line.
