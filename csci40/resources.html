Resources

Everything used in this course is free. No textbook purchase required.

Companion texts (free & open)

  • Elements of AI — University of Helsinki & MinnaLearn. The primary companion: beginner-friendly, no math required, self-paced.
  • Introduction to Artificial Intelligence — Open Textbook Library. Deeper background on history, search, logic, machine learning, and social issues; free PDF.

AI assistants used in class

  • ChatGPT (OpenAI) — free tier is sufficient for this course.
  • Gemini (Google) — also powers AI features in Google Sheets.
  • Claude (Anthropic) — free tier is sufficient for this course.

Practical notes

All tools used in class have free tiers; no student needs to pay for anything. Accounts should be set up with an email you are comfortable using. Never paste private information — yours or anyone else's — into an AI tool; we cover why in Module 6.
