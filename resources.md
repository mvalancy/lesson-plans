# The Resource Library

Games, tools, datasets, and templates. Everything here is free to use, and every link was opened and checked on 22 August 2026.

This is the shelf my AI courses teach from. It was first assembled for Introduction to AI Tools and every course here shares it. Nothing in it is course-only: a teacher, a parent, or anyone curious can pick a game and run with it. Free tiers only, no accounts unless a card says so, and every link fetch-checked the day it was added. Where something is fragile or fussy, the card says that too.

## Free tools by category

The category is the curriculum, the vendor is just today's example. Learn the job (chat assistant, transcription, automation, publishing) and swap the app when a better one shows up. Free-tier terms move constantly, so check before you build a class around one.

| What the skill is | Free options as of mid-2026 |
| --- | --- |
| Chat assistant / general LLM | [Claude](https://claude.ai/), [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/), [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/). Free tiers; pick any, the course never needs a paid one. |
| Local AI (the ownership thread) | [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai/), [Ollama](https://ollama.com/). Fully free, running on your own hardware. |
| Open-model ecosystem | [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/). Free account, thousands of models, Spaces demos you can try in a browser. |
| Design & visual communication | Canva (free plus education), [Figma](https://www.figma.com/) (free starter and free education, now with AI features), Microsoft Designer. |
| Presentations | Gamma free tier, Canva, Google Slides with Gemini. |
| Image generation | Whatever is bundled free in the chat assistants above; Adobe Firefly free tier. |
| Audio: transcription | Otter.ai free tier; Whisper (free and local, ties back to ownership). |
| Audio: voice & music | ElevenLabs free tier, Suno free tier. |
| Video editing | CapCut free tier, Clipchamp (bundled with Windows). |
| Writing polish | Grammarly free tier, plus the AI already built into Gmail, Outlook, and Docs. |
| Research, grounded in sources | NotebookLM (free), Perplexity free tier. |
| Data & notebooks | Google Colab free tier, Google Sheets with Gemini, the free web version of Excel. |
| Automation | [Zapier](https://zapier.com/) free tier, Make free tier, n8n (free if you self-host). |
| Publish & build (the ownership thread) | [GitHub](https://github.com/), [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/), [itch.io](https://itch.io/), [Twine](https://twinery.org/). All free. |

**Before you sign up for anything.** Every tool this course uses has a free tier, so nobody has to pay to take it or teach it. Use an email address you are comfortable using. Never paste private information, yours or anyone else's, into a hosted AI tool. [Module 6](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/intro-ai-tools/lessons/06-ethics-bias.html) covers why, and a model running on your own machine is one honest answer to it.

## Games & interactives

The best part of this library. Sixty-six browser games, simulations, and explorables that teach the course's ideas through your hands instead of through a slide, in the mould of [Survival of the Best Fit](https://www.survivalofthebestfit.com/game/). One game as a warm-up is plenty for a session. Play ten of them across the course and the ethics and verification curriculum lands a second time, quietly.

They also build the judgment employers keep naming: media verification, moderation calls, bias auditing. Grouped by module; the filter narrows the shelf when you already know what you want.

### Module 1 · What is AI?

Prediction, pattern matching, and where the training data came from.

-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/playground-tensorflow-org.webp) M1 · Demystify
    
    #### [TensorFlow Playground](https://playground.tensorflow.org/)
    
    Add layers and neurons, watch the net carve up a field of dots. It is weighted math finding a boundary, with no thinking inside the box.
    
    solo station 10–30 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/roft-io.webp) M1 · Detect
    
    #### [Real or Fake Text](https://www.roft.io/)
    
    Guess the exact word where the human stops writing and the model takes over. Fluency has a seam, and you can learn to feel it.
    
    demo or homework 5–10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/masswerk-at-elizabot-eliza-html.webp) M1 · History
    
    #### [ELIZA (1966)](https://www.masswerk.at/elizabot/eliza.html)
    
    Chat with the original therapist bot. People mistook pattern matching for understanding then, and they still do now.
    
    demo 10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/teachablemachine-withgoogle-com.webp) M1 · Own it
    
    #### [Teachable Machine](https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/)
    
    Train your own webcam classifier in a browser, then feed it a skewed training set and diagnose the failure you caused.
    
    solo station 15–20 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/research-google-com-semantris.webp) M1 · Meaning
    
    #### [Semantris](https://research.google.com/semantris)
    
    Clear word blocks by typing something close in meaning. Embeddings, made playable.
    
    demo 3–5 min
    
    A 2018 Google experiment. Test it the morning of class.
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/semantle-com.webp) M1 · Meaning
    
    #### [Semantle](https://semantle.com/)
    
    Wordle scored by meaning instead of letters. Turns out "similar" is a number.
    
    homework 10–20 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/emojiscavengerhunt-withgoogle-com.webp) M1 · Own it
    
    #### [Emoji Scavenger Hunt](https://emojiscavengerhunt.withgoogle.com/)
    
    Your phone camera hunts real objects on device, nothing uploaded. Capable AI already lives in your pocket.
    
    mobile station 5 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/whichfaceisreal-com.webp) M1 · Detect
    
    #### [Which Face Is Real?](https://whichfaceisreal.com/)
    
    Real photo, or a face the model invented. Generated people are convincing and they do not exist. Fits Module 3 just as well.
    
    demo 3–5 min
    

Human or Not lives on at humanornot.io (the original domain is gone) and Akinator sits behind a bot wall. Both are probably fine, both need a human click before class.

### Module 2 · Everyday Productivity

Prompting as a precision skill: say the thing, say it short.

-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/twinpics-ai.webp) M2 · Precision
    
    #### [Twin Pics](https://twinpics.ai/)
    
    Describe today's image well enough to regenerate it, scored 0 to 100. Bloated descriptions score worse, which is the whole lesson. Free classroom mode, students join with a nickname.
    
    demo or station 10–15 min
    
    A paid classroom tier exists. Confirm the free one before you assign it.
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/artsandculture-google-com-experiment-say-what-you-see-jwg3m7wqshzngw.webp) M2 · Precision
    
    #### [Say What You See](https://artsandculture.google.com/experiment/say-what-you-see/jwG3m7wQShZngw)
    
    The same describe-it-back loop from Google Arts & Culture, free, zero setup, three tries per level. The gentlest way in.
    
    warm-up 5–10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/hackaprompt-com.webp) M2 · Adversarial
    
    #### [HackAPrompt](https://www.hackaprompt.com/)
    
    The deep end of prompt injection, from the Learn Prompting team, with a track for people who have never done it.
    
    stretch homework 30+ min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/semantle-com.webp) M2 · Meaning
    
    #### [Semantle](https://semantle.com/)
    
    The model sees a position in meaning-space, not spelling. That is why swapping one word moves the whole output.
    
    daily warm-up 10–20 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/contexto-me.webp) M2 · Meaning
    
    #### [Contexto](https://contexto.me/)
    
    Same embedding trick, ranked instead of scored. The rotation game once the day's Semantle is spent.
    
    station 10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/research-google-com-semantris.webp) M2 · Meaning
    
    #### [Semantris](https://research.google.com/semantris/)
    
    The arcade version of the same embeddings lesson, for a class that wants speed.
    
    station 10 min
    
    Legacy Google experiment. Test before class.
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/neal-fun-infinite-craft.webp) M2 · Play
    
    #### [Infinite Craft](https://neal.fun/infinite-craft/)
    
    Combine two things and a model invents what you get. There is no fixed answer key, which is the point.
    
    icebreaker 10–15 min
    
    Popular enough to throttle under a whole class. Test with a few devices.
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/play-aidungeon-com.webp) M2 · Play
    
    #### [AI Dungeon](https://play.aidungeon.com/)
    
    Co-write a text adventure and feel the cost of vague instructions in real time.
    
    homework 20–30 min
    
    Free tier is turn-limited. Say so up front.

Making synthetic media, and learning to catch it.

    #### [Which Face Is Real?](https://whichfaceisreal.com/)
    
    Build a checklist of tells: earrings that do not match, warped backgrounds, teeth that go wrong.
    
    demo or station 5–15 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/poloclub-github-io-ganlab.webp) M3 · Mechanism
    
    #### [GAN Lab](https://poloclub.github.io/ganlab/)
    
    Watch a GAN train live in the browser, generator and discriminator fighting over a cloud of dots. The machinery behind image generation, visible.
    
    narrated demo 5–8 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/detectfakes-kellogg-northwestern-edu-study-image.webp) M3 · Detect
    
    #### [Detect Fakes](https://detectfakes.kellogg.northwestern.edu/?study=image)
    
    Judge real against generated, rate your confidence, then see how wrong motivated adults are. Kellogg runs it as live research.

    A real study with an 18+ consent step. Frame it that way.
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/human-ai-collaboration-lab-kellogg-northwestern-edu-same-speaker-or-not.webp) M3 · Detect

    homework 10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/verify-contentauthenticity-org.webp) M3 · Provenance
    
    #### [Content Credentials Verify](https://verify.contentauthenticity.org/)
    
    Drop an image in and read its history: edits, generative tags, capture device. Then note that a screenshot strips all of it.
    
    demo 5 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/autodraw-com.webp) M3 · Create
    
    #### [AutoDraw](https://www.autodraw.com/)
    
    Sketch badly, get a clean icon back. The making side of Quick, Draw!.
    
    warm-up 5 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/artbreeder-com.webp) M3 · Create
    
    #### [Artbreeder](https://www.artbreeder.com/)
    
    Blend images with sliders through latent space. Generation is continuous and remixable, not one shot from a prompt box.
    
    stretch station 10–15 min
    
    Freemium, needs a free account. Flag it before class.
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/twinpics-ai.webp) M3 · Precision
    
    #### [Twin Pics](https://twinpics.ai/)
    
    Recreate the day's image in 100 characters or less, scored on similarity. A recurring warm-up ritual.
    
    warm-up 10 min
    
    Passed our fetch check, blocked one search tool. Click it first.
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/artsandculture-google-com-experiment-say-what-you-see-jwg3m7wqshzngw.webp) M3 · Precision
    
    #### [Say What You See](https://artsandculture.google.com/experiment/say-what-you-see/jwG3m7wQShZngw)
    
    Guess the prompt behind an AI image. A reverse-engineering drill that costs nothing to set up.
    
    warm-up 5–10 min
    

### Module 4 · Spreadsheets & Data

Statistics intuition, and the reminder that people are not numbers.

-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/tylervigen-com-spurious-correlations.webp) M4 · Skepticism
    
    #### [Spurious Correlations](https://www.tylervigen.com/spurious-correlations)

-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/seeing-theory-brown-edu-regression-analysis-index-html.webp) M4 · Intuition

-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/pair-withgoogle-com-explorables-dataset-worldviews.webp) M4 · People
    
    #### [Datasets Have Worldviews](https://pair.withgoogle.com/explorables/dataset-worldviews/)

-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/ncase-me-polygons.webp) M4 · People
    
    #### [Parable of the Polygons](https://ncase.me/polygons/)

### Module 6 · Ethics & Bias

The heart of the shelf. Systems that turn people into numbers, played from the inside.

-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/survivalofthebestfit-com-game.webp) M6 · The anchor
    
    #### [Survival of the Best Fit](https://www.survivalofthebestfit.com/game/)
    
    Automate hiring and watch the model learn your bias faster than you can catch it. The canonical anti-pattern, and the game this whole shelf is modelled on.
    
    whole class 6 min + debrief
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/survivalofthebestfit-com-resources.webp) M6 · Debrief
    
    #### [Best Fit: the resources page](https://www.survivalofthebestfit.com/resources)
    
    The game's own three-part debrief: Behind the Technology, Fair Software, Steps Forward. Ten minutes of structure after the six minutes of play.
    
    debrief 10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/pair-withgoogle-com-explorables-hidden-bias.webp) M6 · Proxies
    
    #### [Hidden Bias](https://pair.withgoogle.com/explorables/hidden-bias/)
    
    Hide the protected attribute from an admissions model and watch its correlated proxies do exactly the same work.
    
    demo 10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/pair-withgoogle-com-explorables-measuring-fairness.webp) M6 · Fairness
    
    #### [Measuring Fairness](https://pair.withgoogle.com/explorables/measuring-fairness/)
    
    Move a screening threshold and watch fairness metrics fight each other. Fair is a choice somebody makes, not a formula you find.
    
    demo 10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/pair-withgoogle-com-explorables-dataset-worldviews.webp) M6 · Categories
    
    #### [Datasets Have Worldviews](https://pair.withgoogle.com/explorables/dataset-worldviews/)
    
    Relabel the categories yourself and watch errors appear and vanish. Whoever names the categories decides what counts as wrong.
    
    demo 10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/pair-withgoogle-com-explorables-measuring-diversity.webp) M6 · Fairness
    
    #### [Measuring Diversity](https://pair.withgoogle.com/explorables/measuring-diversity/)
    
    Hit diversity targets under rules that disagree with each other. Even "diversity" has rival definitions with different consequences.
    
    station 15 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/pair-withgoogle-com-explorables-private-and-fair.webp) M6 · Stretch
    
    #### [Private _and_ Fair?](https://pair.withgoogle.com/explorables/private-and-fair/)
    
    Add privacy noise to a model and watch accuracy fall hardest on the smallest groups. The advanced tier of the PAIR set.
    
    advanced station 15 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/moralmachine-net.webp) M6 · Ethics
    
    #### [Moral Machine](https://www.moralmachine.net/)
    
    Judge self-driving dilemmas, then compare your profile to the world's. Encoding ethics means somebody chose whose life counts.
    
    demo 10–15 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/hownormalami-eu.webp) M6 · Visceral
    
    #### [How Normal Am I](https://hownormalami.eu/)
    
    A real face-analysis pipeline scores your beauty, age, BMI, and life expectancy on camera. The module's warning, felt rather than heard. Processing stays in the browser.
    
    one volunteer 10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/areyouyou-eu.webp) M6 · Surveillance
    
    #### [Are You You?](https://www.areyouyou.eu/)
    
    Try to fool a live face match with props and expressions. Recognition systems are confident well past the point of being right.
    
    station 10 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/moderatormayhem-engine-is.webp) M6 · Labor
    
    #### [Moderator Mayhem](https://moderatormayhem.engine.is/)
    
    Moderate content against a clock until "just remove the bad stuff" falls apart. Moderation is human labor at volume.
    
    station 10–15 min
    
-   ![](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/thumbs/games/trustandsafety-fun.webp) M6 · Labor
    
    #### [Trust & Safety Tycoon](https://trustandsafety.fun/)
    
    Run the whole trust and safety org: staffing, policy, incentives. Moderation is a resourcing problem, not a switch.

## Real datasets

### The climate arc: one theme, four skills

1.  **First look**
    
    NOAA's annual global temperature anomaly is 146 clean rows, the friendliest first upload in the course. Annual Mauna Loa CO₂ is 65 rows.
    
2.  **Chart iteration**
    
    NASA's zonal anomalies build the multi-line chart showing the Arctic warming faster than the globe. Then ask whether the headline exaggerates it.
    
3.  **Fact-check the narrative**
    
    NOAA says one number for 2016 and NASA says another. Both are right, the baselines differ. Berkeley Earth is the third independent source landing on the same trend.
    
4.  **Hand-verify a statistic**
    
    From the raw monthlies, confirm that CO₂ crossed 400 ppm around 2013 to 2016. From the sea-level file, compute a rough trend and compare it to the stated 3.17 mm/yr.
    
5.  **Write your own assessment**
    
    Half a page in the student's own words, citing their own charts and their own verified numbers. The data is public, the judgment is theirs.

Two useful datasets are deliberately too large to upload: [CA employment and wages by industry and county](https://data.ca.gov/dataset/quarterly-census-of-employment-and-wages) and the [College Scorecard](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/) (tuition, debt, and post-graduation earnings per college, which answers the question students actually have about whether a degree pays off). ## Build your own

The origin story is the pitch. Survival of the Best Fit was a class project: four NYU ITP students built it for a course called Interactive Media and the Politics of Code, and it went on to a Mozilla Creative Media Award and classrooms all over the world. Students can finish this course where they started, by making the next one.

### Build paths, lowest floor first

-   **An AI-drafted single HTML file, published.** The course already teaches the pipeline (GitHub to Cloudflare Pages), and [itch.io](https://itch.io/) hosts single-file HTML games free: [their docs](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5) say one HTML file, or a zip with an index.html, up to 500 MB. Lowest floor with AI help, highest ceiling, no new tooling.
-   **[Twine](https://twinery.org/), for anything narrative.** Branching stories in plain text with `[[link]]` syntax. An assistant can draft a whole branching scenario as Twee for a student to paste in, and the macros can track a "bias meter" for the stretch tier. The editor is GPL-3.0 ([source](https://github.com/klembot/twinejs)) and your stories stay yours. _The site bot-blocks fetchers; we verified it through the GitHub source._
-   **[CodePen](https://codepen.io/)** for iterating an AI-drafted snippet live, mid-class. _Bot-blocked during our sweep, though the free tier is well established._
-   **[Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/)** has the lowest syntax floor in existence and is still the wrong tool here: an assistant cannot write block scripts for a student to paste, and data-driven sims get awkward fast. Use it as an accessibility ramp, not the default.

## Own the stack

Optional, and worth an evening of your life. None of this needs a budget, a server, or a computer science degree. A recent laptop or tablet is already enough hardware to run a real model without sending a single word to anybody else.

-   [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/) is the open commons for models and datasets. Browse it once and the phrase "an AI" stops meaning one company's product.
-   **Run it:** [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) or [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai/) is the shortest path from "models are mysterious" to a capable one answering you offline, on your own machine.
-   **Serve it:** when one machine answers many people, [vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm) is the open engine real deployments use, and [LiteLLM](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm) puts one gateway in front of every model you run (the live demo on this site answers through a self-hosted LiteLLM gateway, keys and rate limits included).
-   [GitHub](https://github.com/) plus [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/) or [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) are free ways to put what you make on the open web under a name you control. This site is built that way and its source is public.
-   **Your own hardware.** The ladder is longer than people think. The neural accelerators in current iPads and Macs run real models today. An RTX card turns a gaming PC into a serious inference box. NVIDIA's Jetson AGX Thor puts the same capability in robots, and the DGX Spark (the GB10 chip) is a desktop AI supercomputer. Check what your hardware can already do before renting the same capability by the month.

The [mini-lesson](https://lessons.mattvalancy.com/intro-ai-tools/mini-lesson/) ends with a live terminal wired to a small open model running on private hardware: a worked example of exactly this, and a reminder of how readily small models make things up.
