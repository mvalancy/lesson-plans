A 15-Minute Mini-Lesson

A complete, standalone teaching segment from the course — the style and pacing of CSCI 40 in miniature.

What this is

Every session of CSCI 40 is built from short, focused teaching segments: a clear idea, a live demonstration, and a moment where students try it themselves. This page hosts one complete 15-minute segment as a sample of how the course teaches.

The sample mini-lesson is being prepared and will be published here shortly. It will include the full lesson plan (hook, core idea, live demo, guided practice, and check for understanding) along with its supporting materials.

Candidate topics

Drawn from the course modules, any of these makes a strong standalone 15-minute lesson:

  • From Module 3

    Diffusion models in AI image generation

    How a text prompt and pure noise become a photograph that never happened.

  • From Module 4

    Using AI in Excel / Google Sheets

    From a messy dataset to a clean answer and an honest chart, with AI as copilot.

  • From Module 5

    How LLMs search, hallucination risks, fact-checking

    Why confident answers can be fiction — and a five-minute workflow to catch it.

  • From Module 7

    Workflow automation, Zapier, and AI copilots

    Teach a computer to do a chore: triggers, actions, and an AI in the middle.
