# Activity ideas: Module 3 — Working with AI Images, Audio, and Video

Part of the [activity idea library](../ACTIVITY_CONCEPTS.md) — brainstorming
material, not finished lesson plans. Scaffold tags, floor/stretch notes, and the
three pillars (agency · ownership · humanization) are defined in the top-level map.

> **Deepening this file:** each module file is independent so a research agent can
> go deep on one lesson at a time. When adding ideas: keep the entry format, verify
> every link is live, mark inventions as *(adapted)*, and prefer activities with a
> low floor, a high ceiling, and a workplace or community tie-in.


- **Twin Pics daily prompt challenge** `[assist→solo]` — Recreate a target AI image with a
  ≤100-character prompt; a similarity score gives instant feedback. Great recurring
  warm-up. [twinpics.ai classroom methods](https://twinpics.ai/blog/teach-ai-literacy-classroom-methods)
- **Say What You See (Google Arts & Culture)** `[show→solo]` — Reverse-engineer the
  prompt behind an AI image, compare to the real one. [Central Methodist LibGuide](https://centralmethodist.libguides.com/ai-literacy/prompt_engineering/text-to-image)
- **Prompt ingredient drill** `[assist]` — Rewrite a vague one-liner using a template of
  ingredients (subject, setting, lighting, framing, style); generate both and compare.
  Stretch: derive ingredients from a business brief ("hero image for a bakery site").
  [Central Methodist LibGuide](https://centralmethodist.libguides.com/ai-literacy/prompt_engineering/text-to-image)
- **Image prompt battle** `[solo teams]` — Same theme, time limit, class votes; mirrors
  real design-agency prompt-battle events. [promptbattle.com](https://promptbattle.com/)
- **Draw-then-generate bias check ("a scientist")** `[assist]` — Sketch "a scientist,"
  then generate one; compare demographic defaults. Stretch: cite documented occupation-bias
  studies. [twinpics.ai](https://twinpics.ai/blog/teach-ai-literacy-classroom-methods), [Art Education study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00043125.2024.2330340)
- **Group bias chart (occupation prompts)** `[solo→assist]` — Teams generate "a successful
  CEO," "a nurse," etc., tally demographics class-wide. Stretch: design counter-bias
  prompts and re-test. Workplace: AI-generated recruiting/stock imagery. [twinpics.ai](https://twinpics.ai/blog/teach-ai-literacy-classroom-methods)
- **Style study comparison** `[show→solo]` *(adapted)* — Same subject in 3–4 named styles;
  paragraph on why a business would pick one. Includes the artist-name-in-prompt ethics
  question. [Edutopia on AI in art class](https://www.edutopia.org/article/generative-ai-art-school/)
- **Workplace flyer/logo sprint** `[solo]` *(adapted)* — Time-boxed: AI design tool +
  one-sentence brief → flyer or logo; class critiques layout, legibility, brand fit.
  Stretch: full brand brief + 2-sentence rationale. [Canva Magic Design](https://www.canva.com/magic-design/)
- **AI presentation deck build-and-fix** `[assist→solo]` *(adapted)* — Outline → AI deck,
  then manually fix ≥3 slides and say why; graded on the edits, not the generation.
  [Canva AI presentations](https://www.canva.com/create/ai-presentations/)
- **Social media content batch** `[solo]` *(adapted)* — Caption + AI image + hashtags for
  a fictional event; check brand/tone consistency. Stretch: adapt across two platforms.
- **AI alt-text draft-and-revise** `[assist→solo]` — Generate alt text for 3–4 images,
  then edit for accuracy and context; charts/infographics as the stretch tier. Core
  accessibility-compliance skill. [Maricopa accessibility handbook](https://open.maricopa.edu/accessibilityhandbook/chapter/ai-generated-alt-text/), [JMU guide](https://www.jmu.edu/accessibility/digital-accessibility/fundamentals/guides/image-description/ai-generated-alt-text.shtml)
- **Text-to-speech accessibility remix** `[assist]` *(adapted)* — Run written content
  through TTS; evaluate pronunciation/pacing; stretch: edit the source text to improve
  the AI voice's delivery. [ReadSpeaker](https://www.readspeaker.com/sectors/education/)
- **Transcript accuracy audit** `[assist→solo]` *(adapted)* — Transcribe a short clip with
  Otter/Whisper, hand-correct, categorize error types. Stretch: noisy/jargon audio + build
  a glossary to improve the tool. Workplace: meeting notes, captions. [MSU Denver Otter guide](https://www.msudenver.edu/teaching-learning-design/instructional-accessibility/guides-resources/multimedia/otterai-transcripts/)
- **AI music mnemonic / song remix (Suno)** `[assist→solo]` — Turn your own writing into a
  song; critique tone/genre fit and revise; curation-beats-first-output as the lesson.
  [AVID Open Access on Suno](https://avidopenaccess.org/resource/suno-an-ai-powered-music-generation-tool/), [Edutopia demo](https://www.edutopia.org/video/ai-tool-demo-music-generation-with-suno)
- **Storyboard-to-clip video lab** `[show + short solo]` *(adapted)* — 3-shot storyboard →
  short AI clips; diagnose physics/continuity failures. Demo-heavy given cost/compute.
- **Two Truths & AI (Common Sense Media)** `[show]` — Three movie posters, one fake; spot
  it via text errors and lighting tells. Ready-to-use whole-class tool. [commonsense.org/two-truths-and-ai](https://www.commonsense.org/two-truths-and-ai)
- **MIT Detect Fakes challenge** `[solo→assist]` — 20-image real/deepfake tool + debrief on
  detection strategies and why "no single tell" survives model improvement. [MIT Media Lab](https://medium.com/mit-media-lab/detect-deepfakes-how-to-counteract-misinformation-created-by-ai-4ea111251c5f)
- **"Who owns this image?" copyright debate** `[assist]` *(adapted)* — Stakeholder debate
  (artist / AI company / business user) over "can you use an AI logo commercially?", built
  on real Copyright Office positions and lawsuits. [Sheridan LibGuide](https://sheridancollege.libguides.com/copyright/generative_ai), [PCC copyright guide](https://guides.pcc.edu/copyright/generativeAI)
