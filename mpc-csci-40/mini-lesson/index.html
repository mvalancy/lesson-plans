A 15-Minute Mini-Lesson

A complete, standalone teaching segment from the course — the style and pacing of CSCI 40 in miniature.

What this is

Every session of CSCI 40 is built from short, focused teaching segments: a clear idea, a live demonstration, and a moment where students try it themselves. This page hosts one complete 15-minute segment as a sample of how the course teaches.

Current focus

The mini-lesson now in development is How LLMs search, hallucination risks, and fact-checking — from Module 5. In fifteen minutes: why a confident AI answer can be pure fiction, a live hallucination catch, and a fact-checking workflow anyone can use.

The full lesson plan (hook, core idea, live demo, guided practice, and check for understanding) and its supporting materials will be published here as they are built.
